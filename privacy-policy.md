# Privacy Policy for Rewind for X

**Last updated:** November 14, 2026

Rewind for X ("the Extension") is a Chrome extension that brings back posts you were reading when the X (Twitter) feed refreshes.

## Data collection

The Extension does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal or browsing data.

- It runs only on `x.com` and `twitter.com` pages.
- To restore posts after a refresh, it keeps temporary in-memory copies of up to five posts already shown on the page. These are discarded when you dismiss the banner, leave the page, or reload it, and never leave your browser.
- It does not use `chrome.storage`, cookies, local storage, or any other persistent storage.
- It makes no network requests and does not contact any server, analytics service, or third party.
- It requests no extension permissions beyond running its content script on X pages.

## Children's privacy

The Extension does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.
